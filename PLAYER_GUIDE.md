# The Jinyang Pact — Quick Player Guide

You are a clerk in Jinyang, a city under siege. Shi Jingtang needs help from the Khitan. Speak with the council, review the request for assistance, and make decisions when the game asks you. Other characters act on what they know and want.

## 1. Start a game

Open the game page and click **New session**. For a first try, choose **Use offline demo**, then **Begin**. The demo uses offline mock characters and has no model cost. You can also choose a configuration, mode, and model yourself.

## 2. Take your turn

1. Read the situation, your goal, and the draft on the **Play** page. Watch the grain supply, siege pressure, and rounds left.
2. In **Speak**, choose who should hear you. Write your own words and click **Say**. When several people are present, selecting just one makes the line private; the button becomes **Whisper to…**. Others in the room will not hear it.
3. Need an idea? Open a **Character Profile** and choose a topic under **Openings for your next turn**. This selects that character for a private conversation and shows an **Example — for reference only** beside the input. The example is never inserted or sent. Write your own message; you can close the example or clear it by changing the listener or mode.
4. To change the request, click **Unroll the treaty · Edit terms** under **Request for assistance**. Saving changes creates a new version and voids approval of the current version.
5. When ready, click **End turn →** to let the council deliberate. Ending a turn moves time forward, and the situation may change.

## 3. Make a decision

If you see **A decision is needed**, click **Open the decision**. Read the facts and available choices. Depending on the situation, you may endorse, withhold, amend, or ask someone present a question. Choose an option and click **Decide: …**.

## 4. Send the request

**Transmit…** becomes available when the draft is approved and may be sent. Review the version and every term. Choose **Back to edit** if anything needs changing. Otherwise, click **Confirm and seal**. Your “遣” seal appears only after the server confirms the dispatch. Once sent, the request cannot be recalled.

## 5. Follow the story

The **Play** page shows council conversations and news. Use **Timeline** to review events and **Usage** to see model activity. At the end, you can **Skip** the ending animation and later **Replay ending**.

**Remember:** topic examples do not speak for you, private lines reach only the selected listener, and advice does not make another character's decision for them.
